Quotation

Tenancy

Agency fee is half a month's rental plus 8% SST. Tenancy agreement fee uses the table formula: (rental × 12 ÷ 250, rounded up) × 1 for 1 year or × 3 for 2–3 years, plus RM120 + RM200.

Rental Quotation

Tenancy stamp duty

From the Tenancy Stamp Duty table: annual rental ÷ 250, rounded up, × 1 for a 1-year tenancy or × 3 for 2 or 3 years.
